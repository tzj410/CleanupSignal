# Prospect research notes

`prospects-researched.csv` contains 20 distinct firms whose own websites describe relevant environmental capabilities or a regional presence. Research checked October 23, 2026. Each row includes its official evidence link. This is a research queue, not a customer list; all are uncontacted and purchase intent is unknown.

Priority A means a closer initial service fit. Priority B means a narrower or secondary fit, including water/restoration specialists or multidisciplinary firms whose size or existing intelligence capacity needs qualification. Neither priority establishes that a firm is an SBA small business. Recheck ownership, service scope, certification, active status, contact route, and decision-maker before outreach. Website statements are not independent credential verification.

Start with Urban Green, Path, BP Environmental, Advantage, and ONE for brownfields/assessment relevance. Abatement specialists become more useful once verified abatement solicitations are present. Water/restoration firms should receive samples only when the feed contains that service scope. Do not claim the current initial feed covers all of these firms' services.

Use the public website's contact route to find a business-development contact; no person emails have been guessed or gathered into the file. No outreach was sent. Keep subsequent correspondence, qualified contacts, payments, and delivery notes private. The empty `customer-tracker-template.csv` is intended to be copied into a private location, not filled in this public repository.
