THE EVIDENCE DESK

Know where
the signal came from.

Public sources. Visible limits. No invented deadlines, buyers, or contract values.

What qualifies as a signal?

A documented public project or funding selection with an identifiable recipient, location, and government source. An open solicitation must also have a current notice and future response deadline.

What doesn't it prove?

A grant selection does not establish an available subcontract, a procurement date, or the unspent balance. Recommended actions are CleanupSignal editorial guidance, not agency instructions.

Coverage is a pilot

Initial coverage concentrates on EPA brownfield selections in Maryland and Virginia. DC and state procurement coverage are not complete. A zero count means no published records in this dataset, not no projects.

Your information stays local

Watchlists and pilot drafts stay in your browser or downloaded files. There are no tracking cookies or online payments. Browser storage may be cleared; export your brief to keep a copy.
