What happens to corrections?

We compare the record with the official source. If an open-bid status or deadline cannot be verified, it should be marked unconfirmed while the issue is reviewed. Government amendments control the actual solicitation.

No response-time guarantee is currently offered. For time-sensitive bid questions, contact the procurement authority directly.

Current service availability

The public bid center, reports and downloadable briefing are available without registration. Customer sign-up, payments and automatic subscriber emails are not active. Selecting a plan does not authorize a charge.
